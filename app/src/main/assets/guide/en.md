## Start the day
- Open Cash counter and enter the opening cash (the change in the drawer).
- The dashboard shows Today. Use the chips (Yesterday, This week, This month or a date) to see other days.

## New sale (bill)
- Press New sale. Tap the services the customer took; tap again for 2 pieces.
- Choose "Served by" (the staff member) so commission is counted.
- Tap the customer chip to choose or add a customer (name and phone).
- Press Checkout, add a discount if any, then choose how the customer paid.

## Cash, JazzCash, EasyPaisa, bank
- Settings > Payment accounts: add JazzCash, EasyPaisa, your bank or card machine (quick add buttons).
- At checkout choose Cash or the account the money went to.
- The dashboard card "Money by account" and the Close Day report show how much came into each account.

## Udhaar (credit) sale
- At checkout choose "Udhaar (credit)". Choose the customer first (name and phone are needed).
- Enter what the customer paid now (or leave empty). The rest becomes a pending bill automatically.
- Only the paid part goes into the cash drawer. The receipt shows Paid, Balance (udhaar) and an UDHAAR stamp.

## Pending bills and reminders
- Dashboard > Udhaar shows who owes how much.
- WhatsApp or SMS opens with the reminder ready; you press Send. The app never says "sent" for you.
- When the customer pays, press Received. Tick "Received in cash" to add it to today's cash drawer.

## Receipt: print, WhatsApp, picture
- After a sale the receipt opens. Print it, save PNG / JPEG, share it or send it on WhatsApp.
- Turn on Auto print in Printer settings to print every bill at once.

## Correct a wrong bill
- Open the receipt and press Edit receipt (owner PIN if set). Change services, price, staff, payment or udhaar, then Save changes.
- The receipt number stays. Cash, staff commission and udhaar follow the correction.
- Void cancels a receipt completely (it stays in history as VOID).

## Receipt designs and bold items
- Printer settings > Receipt design: Classic, Modern, Minimal, Elegant, Mono (till slip) or Mono table.
- Services: edit a service and turn on "Bold name" or "Bold price" to make it stand out on receipts.

## Tokens and advance booking
- Settings > Tokens and bookings: turn it on.
- New token gives the next number for today; Book gives a token for a date and time.
- After a token is made the print preview opens: exactly what the printer prints. Print, send WhatsApp text, WhatsApp picture, SMS or Share.
- Call next moves the queue. Today's tokens also show on the dashboard.

## Printer (Bluetooth or LAN)
- Settings > Printer. Choose Bluetooth (pair the printer, select it) or LAN / Wi-Fi (enter the printer IP and port, usually 9100).
- Press Check connection and Test print. Choose 58 mm or 80 mm paper.
- Urdu prints correctly in Image mode. Text mode is fastest but prints English letters only.

## Services and menu with photos
- Services: add the name, category and price. Add a photo of the style, beard or facial.
- Menu shows every service with photo and price by category, like a restaurant menu. Show it to the customer.

## Staff, commission and owner
- Staff: add each worker with salary type (fixed, commission, or both) and commission %.
- If you work yourself, add yourself with role Owner: your work shows apart as "Owner's own work" with no commission.
- Record payment (salary, advance, commission). After saving you can print or share the staff payment slip.

## Expenses
- Expenses: business expenses (rent, bills, products) and personal / household spending are kept apart.
- "Paid from the counter" takes cash expenses out of the cash drawer.

## Close Day
- At night open Close day. Check sales, customers, cash, each account, udhaar, staff commission, owner's work and expenses.
- Count the cash in the drawer and enter it; the app shows short or extra money. Press Close day.
- Print the report on 58 / 80 mm or share it. After closing, new sales count for the next day so the dashboard starts from zero.
- Closed by mistake? Open Close day and press Reopen day.

## Reports, targets and budget
- Reports: sales, profit, top services, staff performance for any period (owner PIN if set).
- Targets: daily / weekly / monthly sales goals with progress on the dashboard.

## Backup
- Settings > Backup: save a backup file (with a password if you like) to your phone, Google Drive or WhatsApp.
- Restore brings everything back on a new phone. Make a backup every week.

## Security PIN
- Settings > Security: set the owner PIN and fingerprint. Reports, expenses, settings and bill corrections then ask for the PIN.

## Language, theme, sound, fast mode
- Settings > App preferences: English, Urdu or Roman Urdu; light or dark theme; colour theme; sound on or off.
- Fast mode: for older phones and rush hours (no intro animation, no screen fades).

## Account and phones
- Your account is approved by DT. A new phone asks the Super Admin for approval.
- The app works offline; it must go online at least once a week.
- Daily sales totals (amount and number of customers only) are shared with DT for billing. Customer names, phones and receipts stay on your phone.

## Help and support
- Help and support: chat with DT. The AI helper can answer at once in your language; a person replies too.
- Clear chat deletes your old messages.

## Problems
- WhatsApp does not open: install or update WhatsApp. The app then opens the browser link or the share menu instead and tells you so.
- Printer does not print: turn the printer on, charge it, keep it near (Bluetooth) or on the same Wi-Fi (LAN), then Test print.
- Dashboard shows zero after Close day: that is normal; new sales count for the next day.

## Din ki shuruaat
- Cash counter kholein aur shuru ka cash (daraz mein khulay paise) likhein.
- Dashboard par Aaj ka hisaab hota hai. Kal, Is hafte, Is mahine ya kisi tareekh ke chip se doosre din dekhein.

## Nayi sale (bill)
- Nayi sale dabayein. Customer ne jo services li hain un par tap karein; 2 dafa ke liye dobara tap.
- "Kis ne kiya" (staff) chunein taake commission bane.
- Customer chip par tap kar ke customer chunein ya naya banayein (naam aur phone).
- Checkout dabayein, discount ho to likhein, phir chunein customer ne kaise paise diye.

## Cash, JazzCash, EasyPaisa, bank
- Settings > Payment accounts: JazzCash, EasyPaisa, bank ya card machine shamil karein (jaldi wale buttons).
- Checkout par Cash ya woh account chunein jis mein paise aaye.
- Dashboard par "Paisa kis account mein" aur Din band report har account ki raqam dikhati hai.

## Udhaar sale
- Checkout par "Udhaar" chunein. Pehle customer chunein (naam aur phone zaroori).
- Abhi jitne paise diye woh likhein (ya khali chhorein). Baqi raqam khud ba khud baqi bill ban jati hai.
- Cash daraz mein sirf diya hua hissa jata hai. Raseed par Ada shuda, Baqi (udhaar) aur UDHAAR ki mohar aati hai.

## Baqi bill aur yaad dihani
- Dashboard > Udhaar: kis ne kitne dene hain.
- WhatsApp ya SMS paigham tayyar khulta hai; Send aap dabate hain. App khud "bhej diya" nahi kehti.
- Jab customer paise de to Wasool dabayein: raqam likhein aur Cash (aaj ke cash drawer mein) ya Online aur JazzCash / EasyPaisa / bank account chunein. Har payment ki list rehti hai; thori thori payment bhi theek hai.

## Raseed: print, WhatsApp, tasveer
- Sale ke baad raseed khulti hai. Print karein, PNG / JPEG save karein, share karein ya WhatsApp par bhejein.
- Printer settings mein Auto print on karein to har bill khud print hoga.

## Galat bill theek karein
- Raseed kholein aur "Raseed mein tabdeeli" dabayein (PIN ho to maange gi). Service, qeemat, staff, payment ya udhaar badlein, phir mehfooz karein.
- Raseed number wohi rehta hai. Cash, commission aur udhaar khud theek ho jate hain.
- Void raseed ko poora cancel karta hai (history mein VOID likha rehta hai).

## Raseed design aur bold
- Printer settings > Raseed design: Classic, Modern, Minimal, Elegant, Mono (saada parchi) ya Mono table.
- Services mein kisi service ka "Naam bold" ya "Qeemat bold" on karein to raseed par numayan hogi.

## Token aur advance booking
- Settings > Token aur booking: on karein.
- Naya token aaj ka agla number deta hai; Booking kisi tareekh aur waqt ka token deti hai.
- Token bante hi print preview khulta hai: bilkul wohi jo printer chhapega. Print, WhatsApp text, WhatsApp tasveer, SMS ya Share.
- "Agla bulayein" se baari aage barhti hai. Aaj ke token dashboard par bhi nazar aate hain.

## Printer (Bluetooth ya LAN)
- Settings > Printer. Bluetooth chunein (printer pair kar ke select) ya LAN / Wi-Fi (printer ka IP aur port, aksar 9100).
- Connection check aur Test print dabayein. 58 mm ya 80 mm kaghaz chunein.
- Urdu Image mode mein sahi chhapti hai. Text mode sab se tez hai magar sirf English haroof.

## Services aur tasveer wala menu
- Services: naam, category aur qeemat likhein. Style, daarhi ya facial ki tasveer lagayein.
- Menu har service tasveer aur qeemat ke saath category wise dikhata hai, restaurant menu ki tarah. Customer ko dikhayein.

## Staff, commission aur malik
- Staff: har worker ki tankhwah ki qisam (fixed, commission ya dono) aur commission % likhein.
- Agar aap khud kaam karte hain to apne aap ko role Malik ke saath shamil karein: aap ka kaam "Malik ka apna kaam" mein alag dikhega, commission nahi.
- Adaigi likhein (tankhwah, advance, commission). Parchi tasveer ki shakal mein khulti hai: Print, WhatsApp (staff ke number par) ya Share. Kisi payment ke raseed icon se parchi dobara khulti hai.

## Akhrajaat
- Akhrajaat: karobari kharche (kiraya, bill, saman) aur ghar ke kharche alag rehte hain.
- "Counter se diya" cash kharch ko cash daraz se nikal deta hai.

## Din band
- Raat ko Din band kholein. Sale, customer, cash, har account, udhaar, staff commission, malik ka kaam aur kharche dekhein.
- Daraz ka cash gin kar likhein; app batati hai kam hai ya zyada. Din band dabayein.
- Report 58 / 80 mm par print karein ya share karein. Band karne ke baad nayi sale agle din mein jati hai, dashboard sifar se shuru.
- Ghalti se band ho gaya? Din band kholein aur "Din dobara kholein" dabayein.

## Report, target aur budget
- Report: kisi bhi muddat ki sale, munafa, top services, staff ki karkardagi (PIN ho to).
- Target: rozana / hafta / mahana sale ka hadaf, dashboard par progress.

## Backup
- Settings > Backup: backup file (chahein to password ke saath) phone, Google Drive ya WhatsApp par mehfooz karein.
- Restore naye phone par sab wapas le aata hai. Har hafte backup banayein.

## Security PIN
- Settings > Security: malik ka PIN aur fingerprint lagayein. Phir report, kharche, settings aur bill ki tabdeeli PIN maangte hain.

## Zubaan, theme, awaz, tez mode
- Settings > App preferences: English, Urdu ya Roman Urdu; light ya dark; rang ka theme; awaz on ya off.
- Tez mode: purane phone aur rush ke waqt ke liye (shuru ki animation aur screen fade band).

## Account aur phone
- Aap ka account DT approve karta hai. Naya phone Super Admin se ijazat maangta hai.
- App offline chalti hai; hafte mein kam az kam ek dafa internet zaroori hai.
- Rozana sale ka total (sirf raqam aur customers ki tadaad) billing ke liye DT ko jata hai. Customer ke naam, phone aur raseedein aap ke phone par rehti hain.
- App sirf location ki ijazat aur location on hone par khulti hai. Phone ki location, model aur app version dukaan ki tasdeeq ke liye DT ko jate hain.

## Madad aur support
- Madad aur support: DT se chat. AI helper foran aap ki zubaan mein jawab de sakta hai; insaan bhi jawab deta hai.
- Chat saaf karein purane paighamat mita deta hai.

## Masail
- WhatsApp nahi khulta: WhatsApp install ya update karein. App phir browser link ya share menu kholti hai aur aap ko batati hai.
- Printer nahi chhapta: printer on karein, charge karein, qareeb rakhein (Bluetooth) ya same Wi-Fi par (LAN), phir Test print.
- Din band ke baad dashboard sifar: yeh theek hai; nayi sale agle din mein shumar hoti hai.
